About Me

I’m Lao David, a Software Engineering student at Limkokwing University of Creative Technology. I enjoy learning through practical projects and building software that solves real-world problems.

I learn best through hands-on projects rather than theory alone. I’m currently strengthening my skills in Java, Spring Boot, MySQL and SQL, REST APIs, Data Structures and Algorithms, Git and GitHub, and Flutter.

My practical experience includes Clinix for a family medicine-distribution business, leading the Petio university project, contributing to team projects such as InvoSight, DSA Java Toolkit and Nudge, and building smaller personal and academic projects.

My goal is to become a backend developer, with a long-term focus on Java, Spring Boot and database-driven systems.
